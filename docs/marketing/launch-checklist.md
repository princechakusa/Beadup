# Launch checklist: first 30 days after App Store approval

Day 1 is the day Apple approves Beadup. **Market in the USA only** (see "Countries" in `README.md`).
Claude drafts everything; the owner approves and posts/sends (nothing goes out without the owner).
Daily habit from Day 3: **20 personal DMs, reply to every comment, help new sign-ups finish setup.**

## Before Day 1 (now, while in review)
- [ ] Buy the domain (`beadup.app` or `getbeadup.com`), point Netlify at it, set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`
- [ ] Domain email: `hello@` and `support@` (set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL`)
- [ ] Brevo SMTP key into Supabase Auth (email sign-in for non-Apple users)
- [ ] Claim the accounts in `accounts.md` (same handle everywhere), add the logo and bio
- [ ] Remove UK and Australia from App Store availability until phone numbers and currency are supported there
- [ ] Set up your own real booking page (services, hours, payment link) to use in every demo

## Week 1: launch
| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_STORE_URL` in Netlify (site buttons switch to Download, Safari banner appears). New promotional text in App Store Connect. Submit the sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. Post launch video 1. |
| 2 | Bio links on every account → website. Pin the launch post. Start the DM list (sheet: name, city, handle, followers, date, reply, follow-up). Find 60 detailers. |
| 3 | DMs 1–20 (`docs/outreach/README.md`, variant A). Post video 2. |
| 4 | DMs 21–40. Join 5 Facebook groups for detailing businesses; read their promo rules. |
| 5 | DMs 41–60. Post video 3. Reply to every DM within an hour while awake. |
| 6 | Follow up Day 3 DMs that opened but didn't reply (one follow-up only). Post a carousel. |
| 7 | Review: replies, sign-ups, finished setups. Grant the free 3 months of Pro to sign-ups (README in `docs/outreach`). |

## Week 2: activation
- Help each sign-up finish: services and prices, hours, payment link, **link in their Instagram bio**, test booking.
- Post videos 4 and 5. Keep 20 DMs a day.
- First Facebook group post (feedback angle) on the group's promo day.
- Ask the most active detailer for a 15-second screen recording of a real booking (offer: we feature their business).

## Week 3: proof
- Post the detailer's recording with their permission, tagging them.
- Contact 5 detailing creators (5k to 50k followers): free Pro for life for an honest review video. No script, no required claims.
- Share one guide in a group thread where someone asks that exact question.
- Ask each detailer with a real booking for an App Store rating (personally, not in bulk).

## Week 4: tighten
- Put the best testimonial on the landing page and in the App Store promotional text.
- Count: sign-ups, finished setups, links in bios, bookings per detailer, Pro subscribers.
- Keep doing what brought sign-ups, drop what didn't. Write guide #5.
- Goal check: **20 detailers with a live link and at least one real booking each.**
