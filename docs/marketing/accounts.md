# Accounts to create (after the domain)

Use **one handle everywhere**, in this order of preference: `beadup` → `beadupapp` → `getbeadup`. Sign up with the
domain email (`hello@<domain>`), not a personal address, and turn on two-factor login for every account. The owner creates and
logs in to every account (agents never create accounts or type passwords).

Profile kit, the same everywhere:
- **Name:** Beadup
- **Photo:** the app icon (`apps/mobile/assets/images/icon.png`)
- **Bio:** `Quote, book and get paid. The app for mobile car detailers. Free on iPhone.` + website link
- **Banner (where there is one):** dark Midnight background, the bead, "Snap a car. Send a quote. Get booked."

## Must have (before launch day)
| # | Account | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Domain email** (Zoho Mail free, or the registrar's mailbox) | `hello@` and `support@`. Needed for sign-ups, App Store support email, Brevo sender |
| 2 | **Instagram** (Professional → Business) | Where mobile detailers live. Main channel for DMs, Reels and outreach |
| 3 | **Facebook Page** | Needed to link Instagram to Meta Business Suite (free scheduling), and to post in groups as Beadup |
| 4 | **TikTok** (Business) | The same short videos reach detailers who aren't on Instagram |
| 5 | **YouTube** channel | Shorts (same videos) plus 2–5 minute setup walkthroughs that rank in Google and YouTube search |
| 6 | **Google Search Console** | Submit the sitemap, see which searches find the site |
| 7 | **Bing Webmaster Tools** | Bing powers ChatGPT search and Copilot answers. Import from Search Console in one click |

## Good to have (week 2 to 4)
| # | Account | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Threads** | Comes free with Instagram; repost the same content |
| 9 | **Reddit** (a personal-looking account, e.g. `beadup_founder`) | r/AutoDetailing and r/sweatystartup. Answer questions honestly, mention Beadup only when asked; Reddit bans hard selling |
| 10 | **X / Twitter** | Reserve the handle; low priority for detailers |
| 11 | **LinkedIn** company page | Credibility for partners and press; low effort |
| 12 | **Buffer** (free) or Meta Business Suite | Schedule a week of posts at once |

## Later
- **Product Hunt** launch (one day, mostly for backlinks and credibility)
- **Pinterest** (before/after pins can drive slow, steady search traffic)
- **Google Business Profile**: not for Beadup itself (it's for businesses that serve customers in person). Tell detailers to
  create one and put their Beadup link on it.
